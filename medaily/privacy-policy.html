Privacy Policy for MeDaily (SelfieADay)

Effective date: 20 June 2025

Data Collection

No Personal Information Collected

MeDaily does not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) about you. We do not ask for your name, email address, contact details, or any account signup/login. You can use the app anonymously without providing personal data.

User Content (Photos & Notes)

Any selfies you take or written thoughts/entries you create within the app are stored locally on your device. These remain in the app’s private storage and are not uploaded to any server or collected by us. Your photos and journal entries stay under your control and are not transmitted off your device unless you explicitly choose to share or export them.

Anonymous Usage Data

To improve the app’s performance and reliability, MeDaily collects anonymous analytics data and crash reports. This means we gather general information about how the app is used (for example, how often certain features are used, or if the app crashes) without any data that personally identifies you. All analytics data is aggregated and anonymized. For instance, we might know that the app had a certain number of users in a week and the general region of those users, but we cannot identify individual users (any location information provided by analytics is approximate). The legal basis for collecting anonymous analytics data is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time via the settings screen. For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), analytics services are only enabled after the user provides explicit consent.

Data Storage and Retention

Local Storage of User Data

All your photos and written entries are stored on your device only. We (the app developers) have no access to this content. It is not uploaded or backed up to our servers (since we do not operate any server for user data). The data remains on your device’s internal storage or app storage space.

Retention of Personal Content

Because your selfies and notes are saved locally, you control how long they are kept. They will remain on your device until you delete them from within the app or uninstall the app. If you uninstall MeDaily, the operating system will typically erase all app data, including your stored photos and entries, as part of the uninstall process.

Analytics and Crash Data Retention

Anonymous technical data (analytics and crash logs) collected through Google Firebase services may be retained by Google according to their own policies. This data includes technical information such as device model, operating system version, app version, timestamps of use, and error logs. We do not store this data on our own servers, but rely on Google’s Firebase/Analytics platforms to handle it. Such data is generally used in aggregate form to monitor performance and will be retained only as long as necessary for analysis and troubleshooting.

Third-Party Services

MeDaily uses a few trusted third-party services (provided by Google) to understand app performance, diagnose issues, and improve your experience. These services may collect anonymous technical data about your device or app usage, as described below. No personal profile information is collected via these services, and you can opt out of them if you wish (see the User Rights and Control section).

Google Analytics for Firebase

We use Google Analytics (via Firebase) to gather insights on how the app is used. This includes aggregate statistics such as the number of daily users, which app screens or features are most popular, and general usage trends. The information collected by Firebase Analytics is anonymous and aggregated – for example, it can tell us the approximate geographic region of users (e.g. city or country) and device information, but not any personally identifying details. This data helps us understand user engagement and make informed improvements to the app’s features.

Firebase Crashlytics

This is a crash reporting tool that helps us quickly find and fix bugs. If the app crashes or encounters an error, Crashlytics will automatically collect a crash report containing technical details about the incident. This report can include information like your device model, OS version, the app’s state at the time of crash, and a stack trace (a record of the code that failed). No personal content from your app (like your photos or notes) is included in these reports. The crash data is used solely to diagnose the problem and improve app stability in future updates.

Google Play Services

MeDaily may rely on Google Play Services (an underlying Google service on Android devices) for certain functionalities or for supporting the above analytics. Google Play Services may in the course of its general operation collect device-level data such as device identifiers or basic usage metrics. We do not control this data collection; however, it is governed by Google’s privacy protocols and is common to most Android apps.

All these third-party services are provided by Google LLC, and data collected through them is handled in accordance with Google’s Privacy Policy. We encourage you to review Google’s privacy terms for more details on how they handle user data. For your reference, here are links to the relevant Google service policies: Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (covered under Google's Privacy Policy). All data communication with these services is securely encrypted in transit (sent over HTTPS) to protect it from interception.

Note: Analytics and crash reporting are optional. We provide a setting in the app that allows you to opt out of these third-party data collections at any time if you prefer not to share any usage data (see User Rights and Control below for details).

Make sure you have read and agreed with Google privacy policy before using the app. Google Privacy Policy

Data Sharing and Disclosure

No Sharing of Personal Data

We do not share, sell, trade, or rent any of your personal data to third parties. In fact, as noted, we do not collect personal identifying information at all. You are not required to provide any personal details to use MeDaily, so there is no personal information of yours that we would have to sell or disclose.

Limited Third-Party Data Disclosure

The only information sent outside of your device is the anonymous analytics and crash data described above, which is transmitted to Google’s Firebase/Analytics servers. This data is used strictly for app performance monitoring and improvement on our side. It is not shared with any other external parties or used for marketing or advertising purposes. Google, in providing the Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics services, does not share the data with additional third parties except as needed for providing those services (for example, Google may process the data on their secure cloud servers or use subprocessors for infrastructure) and as allowed by their terms.

International Data Transfer

Depending on your location, the technical data collected by Google’s services may be processed and stored on servers in a country different from yours (for example, Firebase analytics data might be stored on Google servers in the United States or other jurisdictions). Google handles any such data transfers in compliance with applicable data protection laws and frameworks. By using the app, you understand that this anonymized technical data may be transferred and processed outside of your home country if necessary. Rest assured, no matter where these analytics servers are located, the data does not include personally identifying information.

User-Initiated Sharing

MeDaily provides features for your convenience that allow you to share or export your content only when you choose to do so. For example, you may decide to export your selfie photos to your device’s gallery or share a daily photo/montage to another app (such as social media or messaging apps). Such actions are initiated by you and occur only with your explicit instruction. The app never automatically sends your selfies, thoughts, or any personal content to us or anyone else. When you do choose to share via another app, the data leaves MeDaily and is handled by whatever service or app you choose (subject to that service’s own privacy practices).

User Rights and Control

Opt-Out of Analytics/Crash Reporting

You have the right to disable the collection of anonymous analytics and crash data. An opt-out toggle is provided in the app’s Settings. If you turn off analytics, the app will stop sending any usage statistics or crash reports to our third-party services. You can toggle this on or off at any time. By default, these services are enabled to help us improve the app, but the choice is yours and will be respected.

Access to Your Content

All the data you create in MeDaily (your photos and written entries) is readily accessible to you within the app. You can view your daily selfies and read your past entries at any time. Because this content is stored on your device, no one outside can access it through our app.

Editing and Deletion

You are free to edit or delete your photos and notes at any time. If you want to remove a particular entry or photo, simply delete it within the app and it will be removed from your device’s storage. If you wish to clear all data, you can use any provided in-app delete functions or uninstall the app entirely, which will erase all MeDaily data from your device. Since we do not have any copy of your data on our servers, deleting the app or its data on your device is permanent and removes your content completely.

Permission Controls

The app may request certain device permissions in order to function:

  • Camera: to take your daily selfies.
  • Storage: to save photos on your device and to allow you to export images or backup your entries if you choose.

These permissions are strictly used for the app’s core features. You can always manage these permissions in your device settings. The app will not access your camera or storage without your consent, and it does not use any permissions beyond what is necessary for its functionality.

No Profiling or Advertising

MeDaily does not profile you or serve personalized ads. There are no advertising SDKs tracking you in the app. You will not receive targeted ads based on your usage, and we are not building any profile about you.

User Support Communication

If you contact us for support or feedback (for example, via email), you may provide us with contact information like your email address or other details. We will use this information only to respond to your inquiry or provide assistance. We do not store this information in the app, nor do we add you to any mailing lists without your consent. If you email us, your communication will be kept private and only as long as needed to address your questions.

Security

Data on Your Device

All your personal content (selfies and notes) stays on your device, within the app’s sandboxed storage. This means it's as secure as your device itself. We strongly encourage you to use your device’s security features (such as screen lock, encryption, or biometric locks) to prevent unauthorized access to your phone and the MeDaily app. Since we don’t transmit your personal content over the internet, the risk of external data breaches on our side is eliminated.

Secure Data Transmission

When the app does communicate with our analytics/crash reporting services (Google Firebase), it uses secure, encrypted connections. In other words, any data sent for analytics or crash reports is transmitted over HTTPS, which is an encrypted protocol. This helps protect the data from being intercepted in transit.

No External Servers for Personal Data

We do not store your selfies, thoughts, or any personal entries on any external server. There is no remote database of user content to be hacked or leaked. The design of MeDaily is such that your private content never leaves your device unless you choose to share it.

Data Safeguards

For the limited data that does pass through our third-party services (anonymous analytics and crash logs), we rely on industry-leading providers (Google Firebase) which employ robust security practices. Google Firebase is a trusted service that maintains high security standards for data storage and access. According to Firebase documentation, data collected is encrypted in transit and stored securely on Google’s servers. We keep our integration with these services up to date to benefit from the latest security improvements and patches.

Limitation of Security Guarantees

While we strive to protect your data and privacy, it's important to note that no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. Despite our efforts and the use of reputable services, we cannot guarantee absolute security of information. However, we continuously monitor the app’s security and update our practices to address new threats or vulnerabilities as they arise. If we ever identify a security issue that affects your data, we will act promptly to mitigate it.

Children's Privacy

We are committed to protecting children’s privacy. However, MeDaily is not specifically targeted or marketed to children under the age of 13. It is designed for a general audience. We do not restrict the app’s use by age, but because we do not collect personal data in the app, even young users can use the app without providing personal information.

No Knowing Collection from Children

We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13 years of age. Since the app does not ask for personal details, children should not be able to provide any identifiable info through the app.

Parental Guidance

If you are a parent or guardian, we encourage you to be aware of your child’s use of the app. MeDaily's content (photos and personal journal entries) is user-generated, and there is no offensive or inappropriate material in the app itself. Nonetheless, you may wish to supervise younger users to ensure they understand not to share personal information and to use the app safely.

Contact and Removal

In the event that someone under 13 contacts us directly (for example, by sending an email for support) and provides personal information, or if we inadvertently receive personal data from a user we learn is under 13, we will delete that information as soon as possible. If you believe that a child under 13 may have provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can investigate and delete any such data. We will take prompt action in accordance with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and other relevant laws.
